01 · Citi · Banking & Services Financial Controllers

Enterprise Financial Controllers Build-Out

As Citi moved toward a centralized Enterprise Financial Control (EFC) operating model, there was no consistent way to decide who should own and review manual accounting adjustments. I analyzed the full population of entries, piloted an ownership review on the highest-volume function lead, and turned it into a repeatable framework.

  1. i.Organized the data — structured ledger-adjustment activity in Excel with pivots and lookups.
  2. ii.Profiled & segmented — volume, existing ownership, and gaps across 198 managers, then isolated Banking & Services activity.
  3. iii.Piloted — focused a review on the highest-volume function lead, matching their year-end entries against recent data and asking targeted questions on ownership, recurrence, calculations, accountability, and limitations.
  4. iv.Validated — confirmed roles with controllers and regional managers.
  5. v.Standardized — built a template and targeted review questions for future reviews.

Results

  • Organized and segmented all activity relevant to Banking & Services.
  • Mapped the pilot lead’s year-end entries against recent data to show where each belongs in the new structure.
  • Created a standardized template the team can replicate across the other Banking & Services function leads.
